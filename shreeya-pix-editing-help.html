2How do I remove a background?

  1. Open Shreeya Pix and tap Background removal.
  2. Choose a photo from your library, or take a new one.
  3. Wait for the cutout to finish. Drag the slider on the Result screen to compare the original with the cutout.
  4. Tap Edit to fine-tune, Background to add a new background, or Save & Share when you are done.
For best results, use a clear photo where the subject is separated from the background. Hair, transparent objects and very similar colors can be harder to cut out.

3How do I add a new background?

  1. Create a cutout first.
  2. Tap Background.
  3. Choose None (transparent), White, Black, a Color, a Gradient or a Photo.
  4. Use Move background or Move person to position each layer.
  5. Tap Done, then save the result.

This is useful for profile pictures, product images, posters and other designs.

4How do I fix rough edges?

The Edit tool lets you touch up the cutout with a brush.

  1. On the Result screen, tap Edit.
  2. Choose Erase to remove leftover background, or Restore to bring back parts of the subject.
  3. Set the brush Size, Hardness and Opacity. Turn on Show removed area to see what was removed.
  4. Hold the round handle to paint, tap to move the brush, and pinch to zoom.
  5. Use undo and redo if needed, then tap Done.

5How do I resize an image?

  1. Open Resize image and choose a photo.
  2. Pick Pixels, Percent or Presets.
  3. Choose the output format (JPG, PNG or WebP) and quality. You can also set a maximum file size.
  4. Save the resized image.

6How do I convert photos?

  1. Open Convert format and add one or more photos.
  2. Choose JPG, PNG or WebP and set the quality.
  3. Tap the convert button and save the results.

7How do I merge photos?

  1. Open Merge Images and add photos. Hold and drag to change the order.
  2. Choose a layout: Stack, Side, Grid or Page, and adjust spacing, border and background.
  3. Use To PDF to make a PDF from photos, or PDFs to combine PDF files.
  4. Save the result.

8How do I scan a document?

  1. Open Document scanner and capture a page. Use Add to scan more pages.
  2. Pick a filter: Original, Auto, Magic color or Grayscale.
  3. Use Retake, Rotate, Text or Delete as needed.
  4. Tap Export PDF to save your document.

9How do I save or share my result?

Tap Save & Share after editing to save the image to your device or send it to another app.

  • Allow photo access when asked. If saving fails, check Photos permission in your device settings and make sure you have free storage.
  • Free users may be asked to watch a short ad before saving or sharing. Premium removes ads.
  • When sharing, the app you share to may apply its own rules or compression.

10Common problems

The cutout does not look right

Try a clearer photo with better light and more contrast between the subject and background, then use Erase and Restore to fix the edges.

I cannot save the photo

Check that Shreeya Pix can access your Photos in device settings, and that your device has enough free storage.

Why am I seeing ads?

Shreeya Pix is free and supported by ads. Premium removes ads. See Premium & Purchases.
