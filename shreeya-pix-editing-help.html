2How do I remove a background?

Use the Background Removal feature to separate the main subject from the original background.

  1. Open Shreeya Pix and choose a photo.
  2. Select Remove Background.
  3. Wait for the background removal process to finish.
  4. Review the cutout and make any available adjustments.
  5. Continue to save the result or add a new background.
For best results, use a clear photo where the main subject is reasonably separated from the background.

3How do I remove the background and add my own background?

You can create a cutout and place it over another background image.

  1. Choose the photo you want to edit.
  2. Use Remove Background to create the subject cutout.
  3. Choose the option to change or add a background, where available.
  4. Select a background provided by the app or choose your own image.
  5. Position and adjust the subject as needed.
  6. Save the finished image.

This can be useful for profile pictures, product images, creative photos, posters and other designs.

4How do I erase an unwanted object?

The Object Eraser tool is designed to remove unwanted objects, people or areas from a photo.

  1. Open the photo in Shreeya Pix.
  2. Select Erase Object or the object removal tool.
  3. Brush over or select the unwanted area.
  4. Apply the removal.
  5. Review the result and repeat the process if necessary.
  6. Save the edited photo.
Very large objects, complicated backgrounds or overlapping subjects may require more than one attempt for the best result.

5How do I crop a photo?

The Crop tool lets you remove unwanted edges and keep only the part of the image you need.

  1. Open your photo.
  2. Select Crop.
  3. Adjust the crop area.
  4. Choose an available aspect ratio if needed.
  5. Apply the crop and save the result.

6How do I make an ID photo?

Use ID Photo Maker to prepare a photo in an ID-style layout.

  1. Choose a clear portrait photo.
  2. Open ID Photo Maker.
  3. Select the required photo size or layout, when available.
  4. Adjust the crop and position of the face or subject.
  5. Choose a suitable background if the feature provides that option.
  6. Save the finished ID photo.
Photo size and background requirements can differ by country, application or organization. Always check the requirements of the place where you will submit the photo.

7How do I convert a photo?

Use the Photo Converter to change an image into another supported image format.

  1. Select the photo you want to convert.
  2. Choose Convert Photo.
  3. Select the available output format.
  4. Confirm the conversion.
  5. Save the converted image.

The formats available depend on the current version of Shreeya Pix and your device.

8How do I save or share my edited photo?

After completing an edit, use the app's save option to store the result on your device. You can also use the Share option to send the image to another supported app.

  • Make sure Shreeya Pix has permission to access your Photos or photo library when required.
  • If you cannot save a result, check the app's photo permissions in your device settings.
  • When sharing, the selected third-party app may apply its own rules or compression.

9Common editing problems

Background removal does not look correct

Try a clearer photo with better lighting and stronger separation between the subject and background. Complex edges such as hair, transparent objects and very similar colors can be harder to process.

Object erasing leaves an unwanted mark

Try selecting a smaller area or repeat the erase operation. Results can depend on the size and complexity of the surrounding image.

I cannot save the edited photo

Check that Shreeya Pix has permission to access your Photos or photo library and make sure your device has enough free storage.

The converted photo is not where I expected

Check your device's Photos or the location used by the app for saved files. If the problem continues, contact support.
