1Your photos

Shreeya Pix ("the app", "we", "us") removes backgrounds, erases objects, crops, resizes and converts pictures. These editing operations are designed to happen on your device. Your photos, masks and finished cutouts are not uploaded to our servers by the app, and we do not have access to them through our own service.

The developer of the app is Bhupendra Dahal, who is responsible for the information handling described in this policy.

On Android, the first use may download a recognition model through Google Play services. After that, background removal works offline.

2Permissions and why we use them

Permission or access When it is used What happens to the data
Camera Only when you tap "Take Photo", to capture a picture that you want to edit. The picture is opened in the editor on your device. The app does not use the camera in the background or record video or audio.
Photo library (read) When you choose an existing picture to edit or to use as a custom background. The selected picture is processed on your device. The app does not scan or upload the rest of your library.
Photo library (save) When you choose to save an edited result to your Photos. The finished image is written to your photo library.
Network access To load and measure ads, use Firebase Analytics, verify a Premium subscription, and (on Android) download the recognition model. Information may be exchanged with the relevant service providers as explained in this policy. Your photos are not uploaded to us by the app.

The app does not request access to your microphone, contacts, calendar, precise location or health data for its described features. You can turn camera and photo permissions off at any time in your phone settings. Some features that need them will then stop working.

3What information is collected

We do not ask for, and do not store on our own servers, your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts or photos for the app's editing features. The app does not require a user account.

Data kept on your device may include:

  • Your projects and edited pictures (visible in History).
  • Small app settings and preferences, including your ad-consent choice and whether Premium is active.

Information processed by third-party services may include:

  • Firebase Analytics information: app interaction and usage events, app instance identifiers, app version, device information and related technical information. The exact information depends on the Firebase SDK, its configuration and the events enabled in the app.
  • Advertising information: collected by Google AdMob for users who receive ads, which may include device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions.
  • Purchase information: held by Apple or Google when you subscribe, including subscription status. We do not receive your payment card details.

You can delete projects in History or Settings. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored app data, but does not itself delete information already processed by third-party service providers.

4Google Firebase Analytics

Shreeya Pix uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how people use the app and to help us improve its features and user experience. Analytics may record app events such as first app launch, app updates, screens or features used, and other interactions that are configured in the app.

Depending on the Firebase SDK version, app configuration and events enabled, Firebase Analytics may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • App usage and interaction events, including app launches and screen views where enabled.
  • An app instance identifier or other identifiers used by the Analytics service.
  • App information, such as app version and installation-related information.
  • Device and technical information, such as device model, operating system version, language and general technical signals.
  • General location information inferred from an IP address, where processed by Google as part of providing the service. We do not use Firebase Analytics to collect your precise GPS location.

We use Analytics information to understand general usage patterns, measure feature engagement, and make decisions about app improvements. We do not use Firebase Analytics to collect or upload the photos you edit.

Analytics information is processed by Google on our behalf in accordance with Google's service terms and privacy documentation. Google may process information on servers in other countries. Data retention settings and the information collected can depend on our Firebase project configuration and the SDK behavior.

Learn more: Firebase privacy and security, Google Analytics for Firebase, and Google Privacy Policy.

This section describes Firebase Analytics only. Shreeya Pix does not use Firebase Crashlytics as part of the analytics service described in this policy.

5How we use information

  • To let you edit, save, convert and share your pictures on your device.
  • To understand app usage through Firebase Analytics and improve app features and the user experience.
  • To show ads in the free version and measure whether ads are working.
  • To confirm whether Premium is active so that ads can be turned off.
  • To respond when you contact us for support.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the legal basis for processing may include consent where required, performance of a contract for providing app or Premium features, and legitimate interests for appropriate service operation and improvement. The applicable basis depends on the specific processing and local law.

6Ads and consent

Shreeya Pix is a free app supported by advertising, with an optional Premium subscription that removes ads. Free users may see ads provided by Google AdMob, including banners, native ads, interstitial ads and optional rewarded videos. While Premium is active, ads are not shown.

To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID on Android or IDFA on iPhone where permitted and available.
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information.
  • Ad interactions, such as ads shown, clicked or watched.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we use Google's consent tools where required to request and manage consent for applicable advertising technologies. Available choices depend on the app's implementation and applicable law.

On iPhone, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt may appear if the app requests permission to track. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the app must respect that choice and may not use IDFA for tracking.

Read how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy .

7Premium and purchases

Shreeya Pix offers an optional auto-renewing subscription, Shreeya Pix Premium, that removes ads from the app. The app remains free to use without it.

  • Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your card number or store account password.
  • The app checks with the store or its purchase service whether your subscription is active so it can turn ads off. The app may receive limited purchase information, such as subscription status.
  • Subscriptions are managed, cancelled and refunded through your store account, not through us. See Premium & Purchases for pricing and details.

Apple and Google handle payment information under their own privacy policies.

9Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. We use service providers such as Google Firebase Analytics and Google AdMob for the purposes described in this policy. Those providers may process information to provide their services and as described in their own privacy documentation.

We may disclose information if required by applicable law, legal process or a valid governmental request.

When you use Share, the picture is sent to the app or service you choose (for example, a messaging app). That service's own privacy policy applies.

10Your choices

  • Ads: you can manage available advertising and consent choices through the app's privacy settings where provided, or remove ads with Premium.
  • iPhone tracking: review app tracking permissions in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Firebase Analytics collection and any available analytics controls depend on the app's implementation and configuration. Contact us using the details below with questions about analytics information.

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

13Security and retention

Your projects stay in the app's private storage on your device and are kept until you delete them or remove the app.

We do not hold your photos on our servers, so we do not retain your photos or edited images on our systems. Because editing happens on your device, your photos are protected by your device's own security features.

Information processed by Google Firebase Analytics, Google AdMob, Apple and other service providers is retained according to the relevant provider's policies and, where applicable, the settings configured for the service. Firebase Analytics retention settings may be configurable in the Firebase project. If you email us, we keep the message only as long as reasonably needed to handle your request and meet applicable obligations.

14International transfers

Google, Apple and other service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, appropriate legal safeguards are used for these transfers. We do not upload your photos to our servers through the app's editing features.
