2Common questions

  • Are my photos uploaded? No. Shreeya Pix edits and scans on your device.
  • How do I save an edited image? Tap Save & Share and allow Photos access when asked.
  • Why are ads shown? Shreeya Pix is free and supported by ads. Premium removes ads.
  • What does Premium include? Premium is an optional subscription that removes ads. It does not unlock other tools.
  • How do I restore or cancel Premium? See Premium & Purchases.
  • Can I control personalized ads? Depending on your location and device, you can manage consent in the app and tracking in your device settings.

3About Shreeya Pix

Shreeya Pix is a photo and document app for background removal, background replacement, resizing, format conversion, merging photos and scanning documents to PDF, all on your device.

It is free to download and supported by ads. An optional Premium subscription removes ads.
