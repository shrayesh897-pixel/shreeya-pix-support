1Your photos

Shreeya Pix ("the app", "we", "us") removes backgrounds, erases objects, crops, resizes and converts pictures. All of this happens on your device. Your photos, masks and finished cutouts are not sent to our servers, and we cannot see them.

The developer of the app is Bhupendra Dahal, who is the controller of the information described in this policy.

On Android, the first use may download a recognition model through Google Play services. After that, background removal works offline.

2Permissions and why we use them

Permission When it is used What happens to the data
Camera Only when you tap "Take Photo", to capture a picture that you want to edit. The picture is opened in the editor on your device. It is not uploaded, and the app does not use the camera in the background or record video or audio.
Photo library (read) When you choose an existing picture to edit or to use as a custom background. The selected picture is processed on your device only. The app does not scan or upload the rest of your library.
Photo library (save) When you choose to save an edited result to your Photos. The finished image is written to your photo library. Nothing is sent to us.
Network access To load and measure ads, verify a Premium subscription, and (on Android) download the recognition model. See the Ads and Premium sections. Your photos are never sent over the network by the app.

The app does not request access to your microphone, contacts, calendar, precise location or health data. You can turn the camera and photo permissions off at any time in your phone settings. Some features that need them will then stop working.

3What we collect

We do not ask for, and do not store on our own servers: your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts or photos. The app has no user accounts.

Data kept only on your device:

  • Your projects and edited pictures (visible in History).
  • Small app settings and preferences, including your ad-consent choice and whether Premium is active.

Data collected by third parties when you use the app (see the sections below):

  • Advertising data collected by Google AdMob for free users: device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions.
  • Purchase information held by Apple or Google when you subscribe: that a subscription exists and its status. We do not receive your payment card details.

You can delete projects in History or Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored app data.

4How we use information

  • To let you edit, save, convert and share your pictures on your device.
  • To show ads in the free version and to measure whether ads are working.
  • To confirm whether Premium is active so that ads can be turned off.
  • To respond when you contact us for support.
  • To comply with the law and to protect the app against misuse.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for personalized advertising, on contract for providing the app and Premium, and on our legitimate interests for supporting and securing the app.

5Ads and consent

Shreeya Pix is a free app supported by advertising, with an optional Premium subscription that removes ads. Free users see ads provided by Google AdMob, including banners, native ads, interstitial ads and optional rewarded videos. While Premium is active, ads are not shown.

To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iPhone, only if you allow tracking).
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information.
  • Ad interactions, such as ads shown, clicked or watched.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before ads are requested, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice later where the app provides that option.

On iPhone you may also see Apple's tracking prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized using your IDFA.

Read how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy .

6Premium and purchases

Shreeya Pix offers an optional auto-renewing subscription, Shreeya Pix Premium, that removes ads from the app. The app remains free to use without it.

  • Purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your card number, billing address or store account password.
  • The app checks with the store whether your subscription is active so it can turn ads off. The store may share limited purchase information with us, such as whether a subscription is active.
  • Subscriptions are managed, cancelled and refunded through your store account, not through us. See Premium & Purchases for pricing and details.

Apple and Google handle purchase data under their own privacy policies.

8Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising ourselves. Advertising data is processed by Google as described above.

We may disclose information if required by applicable law, legal process or a valid governmental request.

When you use Share, the picture goes to the app you choose (for example a messaging app). That is your choice, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

9Your choices

  • Ads: you can manage available advertising and consent choices through the app's privacy settings where available, or remove ads with Premium.
  • iPhone tracking: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: Settings > Privacy > Ads, then delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • Premium: subscribe to remove ads, or manage and cancel your subscription in your Apple Account or Google Play settings.
  • Permissions: turn camera or photo access off in your phone settings.
  • Your pictures: delete projects in History or Settings, or uninstall the app to remove all local app data.

10Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or California privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA, you may have rights to know what personal data is collected, to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent, and to not be discriminated against for exercising your rights. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Because we do not keep your photos or personal account information on our servers, most of your data is already under your control on your device.

For data processed by Google for advertising, you can use Google's available privacy controls. For other privacy requests, contact us using the information in the Contact section. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

11Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

12Security and retention

Your projects stay in the app's private storage on your device and are kept until you delete them or remove the app.

We do not hold your photos on our servers, so we do not retain your photos or edited images on our systems. Because processing happens on your device, your photos are protected by your device's own security.

Data processed by Google, Apple and other service providers is retained according to their respective policies. If you email us, we keep the message only as long as needed to help you.

13International transfers

Google, Apple and other service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, they rely on legal safeguards for these transfers. We do not transfer your photos anywhere.

14Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app where appropriate.
