1Your photos and documents

Shreeya Pix ("the app", "we") removes backgrounds, resizes, converts and merges pictures, and scans documents to PDF. All of this happens on your device. Your photos, masks, scans and finished results are not sent to our servers, and we cannot see them.

  • Camera: used only when you tap "Take Photo" or use the document scanner, to capture a picture you want to edit or scan.
  • Photo library: used when you choose a picture to edit, and when you choose to save a result to your Photos.
  • You can turn these permissions off at any time in your phone settings.
Editing, scanning and saving work without sending your photos to us. An internet connection is only needed for ads, Premium purchases and update checks.

2What we collect

We do not ask for, and do not store on our own servers: your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos. The app has no account or sign-up.

Data kept only on your device:

  • Your projects and edited pictures (visible in History).
  • Small app settings and preferences.
  • Whether Premium is active, as confirmed by the App Store.

You can delete projects in History or Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored app data.

3Ads and consent

Shreeya Pix is free to download and is supported by advertising. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including banners, native ads, interstitial ads and rewarded videos. Premium subscribers do not see ads.

To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you allow tracking.
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information.
  • Ad interactions, such as ads shown, clicked or watched.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before ads are requested, using Google's consent tool.

You may see Apple's tracking prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your IDFA is not used to personalize ads.

Read how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4Purchases and Premium

Shreeya Pix offers an optional Premium subscription that removes ads. Purchases are made and billed by the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your card number or other payment details.

The store tells the app whether your subscription is active, so the app can turn ads off. We do not create an account for you and do not collect your name or email for this.

See Premium & Purchases for prices, renewal, cancellation and restore instructions, and the Terms of Use.

6Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising ourselves. Advertising data is processed by Google as described above.

We may disclose information if required by applicable law, legal process or a valid governmental request.

When you use Share, the picture goes to the app you choose (for example a messaging app). That is your choice, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

7Your choices

  • Ads: you can manage advertising and consent choices through the app's privacy settings where available, or remove ads with Premium.
  • Tracking: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Permissions: turn camera or photo access off in your phone settings.
  • Subscription: manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

8Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, UK GDPR or applicable California privacy laws, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent.

Because we do not keep your photos or personal account information on our servers, most of your data is already under your control on your device.

For data processed by Google for advertising, you can use Google's available privacy controls. For other privacy requests, contact us using the Contact section.

9Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

10Security and retention

Your projects stay in the app's private storage on your device and are kept until you delete them or remove the app.

We do not hold your photos on our servers, so we do not retain your photos or edited images on our systems.

Data processed by Google, Apple and other service providers is retained according to their respective policies. Some providers may process data in countries other than yours.

11Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app where appropriate.
