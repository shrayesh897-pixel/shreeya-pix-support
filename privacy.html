1Your photos

Shreeya Pix ("the app", "we") removes backgrounds, resizes and converts pictures. All of this happens on your device. Your photos, masks and finished cutouts are not sent to our servers, and we cannot see them.

  • Camera: used only when you tap "Take Photo", to capture a picture you want to edit.
  • Photo library: used when you choose a picture to edit, and when you choose to save a result to your Photos.
  • You can turn these permissions off at any time in your phone settings.
On Android, the first use may download a recognition model through Google Play services. After that, it works offline.

2What we collect

We do not ask for, and do not store on our own servers: your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos.

Data kept only on your device:

  • Your projects and edited pictures (visible in History).
  • Small settings, such as whether you rated the app or your Premium status.

You can delete projects in History or Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

3Ads and consent

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob (banners, native ads, interstitial ads and optional rewarded videos). To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iPhone, only if you allow tracking).
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information.
  • Ad interactions, such as ads shown, clicked or watched.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before ads are requested, using Google's consent tool. On iPhone you may also see Apple's tracking prompt; if you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized with your IDFA.

Read how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Premium removes all ads.

4Subscriptions and purchases

Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly). Payments are handled entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. We only receive whether a subscription is active, so we can turn ads off.

  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Manage or cancel any time in Subscriptions in your Apple ID or Google Play account.
  • Use "Restore purchases" in the app if you change phones.

6Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising ourselves. Ad data is processed by Google as described above. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

When you use Share, the picture goes to the app you choose (for example a messaging app). That is your choice, and that app's own policy applies.

7Your choices

  • Ads: change your consent in the app under Settings (Privacy settings, where available), or buy Premium to remove ads.
  • iPhone tracking: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: Settings > Privacy > Ads, then delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • Permissions: turn camera or photo access off in your phone settings.

8Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its processing, and to withdraw consent.

Because we do not keep personal data about you on our servers, most of your data is already in your hands on your device. For data held by Google for ads, use Google's privacy controls. For anything else, contact us (section 12) and we will respond within a reasonable time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will help.

10Security and retention

Your projects stay in the app's private storage on your device and are kept until you delete them or remove the app. We do not hold your photos, so there is nothing for us to retain or lose on a server. Data processed by Google and the app stores is kept according to their own policies. Some of these providers may process data in countries other than yours.

11Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
