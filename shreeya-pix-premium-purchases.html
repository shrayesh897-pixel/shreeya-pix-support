1What is Premium?

Shreeya Pix is free to download and use, supported by ads. Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription that removes all ads from the app.

Premium does not unlock extra editing tools. All editing tools are available to everyone.

Payments and subscriptions are handled by the Apple App Store. Shreeya Pix does not receive or store your card or payment details.

3What does Premium include?

  • No ads: banners, native ads, interstitials and rewarded video prompts are turned off.
  • Restore on new devices: sign in with the same store account and restore your purchase.

Background removal, resizing, converting, merging and document scanning work for everyone. Premium only removes ads.

4How do I purchase Premium?

  1. Open Shreeya Pix and tap Go Pro.
  2. Select Monthly ($9.00) or Yearly ($70.00).
  3. Confirm with your Apple Account (Face ID, Touch ID or password).

5How do I restore my purchase?

If Premium is not active after reinstalling or changing device:

  1. Sign in to the same Apple Account used for the purchase.
  2. Open Shreeya Pix, tap Go Pro, then tap Restore Purchases.
  3. Wait for the store to confirm your purchase.
Restoring does not charge you again.

6How do I cancel?

Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription. Cancel in your Apple Account:

Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions, select Shreeya Pix, then tap Cancel Subscription. You can also do this in the App Store app by tapping your profile picture, then Subscriptions.

After you cancel, Premium normally stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

7What if I change devices?

Your purchase belongs to your Apple Account, not to one device. Use the same Apple Account on the new iPhone or iPad and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases cannot be moved between different Apple Accounts.

8I was charged but Premium is not active

  1. Check you are using the same Apple Account that made the purchase.
  2. Open Shreeya Pix and tap Restore Purchases.
  3. Close and reopen the app, with an internet connection.
  4. If it still does not work, contact support with the purchase date and your Apple order number.
Never send your card number or any password to support.

9Billing, refunds and price changes

Who handles billing?

Apple handles payment, billing and your payment method. We cannot see your card details.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are decided by Apple. Use Apple's "Report a Problem" page (reportaproblem.apple.com) or contact Apple Support.

Does cancelling refund me?

No. Cancelling stops future renewals. It does not refund the current period.

Price changes

If the price changes, the store will notify you and may ask for your consent before you are charged the new price.
