1What is Premium?

Shreeya Pix Premium is an optional paid subscription that provides an ad-free experience while using the app.

Premium is available as a $11.99 monthly plan or a $100.00 yearly plan.

Payments and subscription management are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Shreeya Pix does not receive or store your credit-card or payment details.

2What features are included?

  • Ad-free experience: Premium removes advertisements from Shreeya Pix.
  • Use the app without ads: Continue using the app's editing features without advertising interruptions.
  • Premium access across supported devices: Your purchase can be restored through the store account used to make the purchase.

Shreeya Pix's editing features include background removal, resizing and image conversion. Premium is primarily provided to remove ads from the app.

3How do I purchase Premium?

You can purchase Premium directly inside the Shreeya Pix app.

  • Open Shreeya Pix.
  • Open the Premium or subscription screen.
  • Select Monthly ($11.99) or Yearly ($100.00).
  • Complete the purchase using the payment method connected to your Apple App Store or Google Play account.

The store may display the final price in your local currency and may apply applicable taxes or other charges.

4How do I restore my purchase?

If you previously purchased Premium and it is not showing as active, use the Restore Purchases option in Shreeya Pix.

  • Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google Play account used for the purchase.
  • Open Shreeya Pix and go to the Premium screen.
  • Tap Restore Purchases.
  • Wait for the purchase to be verified by the store.
Restoring a purchase does not charge you again for an eligible previous purchase.

5How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the store where you purchased Premium. Deleting the app does not automatically cancel an active subscription.

Apple App Store

On iPhone or iPad, open Settings > your Apple Account > Subscriptions, select Shreeya Pix, and follow the cancellation instructions.

Google Play

Open Google Play, go to your profile, select Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions, choose Shreeya Pix, and follow the cancellation instructions.

If you cancel, you can normally continue using Premium until the end of the already-paid subscription period. Store subscription rules apply.

6What happens if I change devices?

If you move to another supported iPhone, iPad or Android device, you may be able to restore your Premium purchase using the same store account that made the purchase.

  • Apple: use the same Apple Account that purchased the subscription.
  • Android: use the same Google Play account that purchased the subscription.
  • Open Shreeya Pix and use Restore Purchases if Premium does not appear automatically.

Purchases are tied to the applicable Apple App Store or Google Play account and are not transferred between different store accounts.

7I was charged but Premium is not active

If you were charged but Premium is not active, please try these steps:

  1. Confirm that you are using the same Apple Account or Google Play account that made the purchase.
  2. Open Shreeya Pix and use Restore Purchases.
  3. Close and reopen the app.
  4. Make sure your device has an internet connection so the purchase can be verified.
  5. If Premium still does not activate, contact Shreeya Pix Support with your purchase information.
Please do not send your full credit-card number, password, Apple Account password or Google Account password to support.

8Billing & refund questions

Who handles billing?

Apple App Store or Google Play handles payment processing, billing and the payment method associated with your purchase. Shreeya Pix does not see or store your card details.

How do I request a refund?

Refund requests are handled by the store through which you made the purchase. If your purchase was made through Apple, use Apple's purchase/refund support. If it was made through Google Play, use Google Play's purchase/refund support.

Will cancelling give me a refund?

Cancellation normally stops future renewal; it does not automatically mean that the current subscription payment is refunded. Refund eligibility is determined by the applicable store's policies.
