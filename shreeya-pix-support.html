Common Questions

  • Are my photos uploaded? No. Shreeya Pix processes your photos on your device.
  • How do I save an edited image? Use the save option in the app and allow Photos access when requested.
  • How do I restore Premium? Open the purchase or Premium screen in the app and choose “Restore Purchases”.
  • How do I manage my subscription? Manage or cancel it from your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings.
  • How do I report a problem? Email us and include your device model, operating system version, and a short description of the issue.

About Shreeya Pix

Shreeya Pix is a photo editing app designed for background removal, resizing, image conversion and creating clean cutouts directly on your device.
